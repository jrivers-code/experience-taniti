Taniti Activities

Explore

Trails

Explore Taniti's lush tropical rainforest, mountainous landscapes, and active volcano. Visitors can enjoy scenic hikes, thrilling rainforest zip-lining adventures, and helicopter rides offering spectacular views of the island. For a more relaxed experience, discover Taniti's local history museum and art galleries to learn more about the island's culture and heritage.

Beaches

Relax along Taniti's beautiful white, sandy beaches surrounding Yellow Leaf Bay, or explore the island's tropical waters through snorkeling. After a day at the beach, visit Merriton Landing for entertainment, including pubs, a microbrewery, a dance club, movie theater, arcade, and bowling. A nine-hole golf course is also expected to open next year.

Boats

Experience Taniti from the water with scenic boat tours and chartered fishing excursions. Visitors can explore the island's coastline and enjoy views of its tropical landscape. For those who prefer sightseeing on land, bus tours provide another way to discover the island, including Taniti City, known for its native architecture and nearby beaches.
