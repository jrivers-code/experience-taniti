Where to Stay in Taniti

Stay

Hotels & Resorts

Taniti offers a variety of hotel accommodations, including small family-owned hotels and one large four-star resort. Visitors can choose lodging that fits their preferences and travel needs. All lodging is regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.

Bed & Breakfasts

Taniti has a growing number of bed and breakfasts for visitors looking for an alternative to traditional hotels. These accommodations provide another option for travelers exploring the island. Like other lodging on Taniti, bed and breakfasts are regulated and regularly inspected.

Hostels

Travelers looking for inexpensive accommodations can consider Taniti's hostel. This option allows visitors to spend less on lodging while enjoying the island's beaches, rainforest, and attractions. The hostel is also subject to government lodging regulations and inspections.
