Dining & Shopping in Taniti

Dine & Shop

Dining

Taniti has 10 restaurants offering several dining options. Five restaurants primarily serve local fish and rice dishes, three serve American-style meals, and two offer Pan-Asian cuisine. Visitors can enjoy different flavors while exploring the island.

Shopping

Visitors can purchase groceries and everyday necessities at Taniti's two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one convenience store that operates 24 hours a day. These options make it convenient for travelers to purchase supplies during their stay.
